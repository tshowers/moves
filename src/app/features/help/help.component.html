What is Moves?

An assistant for the person who keeps the team moving

Work rarely fails because nobody wrote it down. It fails because it goes still: a move has no owner, nobody knows the next step, or something starts before the thing it waits on. Moves makes those breaks visible to the project lead, and TODD fixes the small ones on its own. It answers:
